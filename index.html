In the medina
Where to go today
Skala de la Ville
Ramparts · 10 min walk from Place Moulay Hassan
Old cannon-lined sea wall — best light just before sunset, quiet in the morning.
Skala du Port
Fishing port · south end of the medina
Watch the blue boats unload — grilled fish stalls right there, cheapest lunch in town.
Souk aux Épices
Spice & thuya market · central medina
Argan oil, saffron, thuya wood boxes. Bargain, and buy from the back stalls not the entrance.
Essaouira Beach
South of the ramparts · wind picks up after noon
Go early for calm water and horse rides; afternoons belong to the kitesurfers.
Sidi Kaouki
Day trip · 25 min south by car
Quiet surf village with camel walks on the beach — half-day is enough, ask a driver in Contacts.
